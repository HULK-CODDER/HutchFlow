What does RV mean

At CN, an RV number is a reservation/appointment reference assigned to a container transaction.

For example:

Container: MSCU1234567
Pickup: 123456
Terminal: CN Brampton
Appointment: 14:00–15:00
RV: RV12345678
Your driver uses that RV/appointment information when going to the terminal.
CN explains that each container included in an appointment receives a unique RV number
