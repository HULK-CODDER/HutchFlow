# 🚢 HutchFlow (HUTCH Workstyle)
> **Training Platform for Dispatch Operations**

HutchFlow is an interactive web-based training platform designed for container dispatch operations, logistics management, and freight coordination. The platform provides a structured, multi-phase workflow to train dispatchers and staff on efficient, reliable, and timely container movement.

---

## 🌟 Core Mission & Services

HutchFlow optimizes supply chain and freight forwarding operations by focusing on:
- **Driver & Equipment Support:** Providing professional drivers alongside proper chassis to ensure smooth, safe container transportation.
- **Container Pickup & Delivery:** Managing the full lifecycle from picking up containers at rail/shipping terminals to delivering them to consignees and returning empty containers.
- **Dispatch Coordination:** Assigning loads, tracking movements, and bridging real-time communication between drivers, terminals, and customers.

---

## 🔄 The Load Cycle (System Logistics)

The platform outlines the complete lifecycle of a containerized load from origin to destination:
1. **Origin & Shipping:** Containers are packed at the shipper's facility and transported via international shipping lines.
2. **Import & Supply:** Containers are transferred to rail networks for regional distribution (e.g., across Canadian provinces).
3. **Arrival at Terminal:** Containers arrive at regional rail terminals (such as those in Ontario) for pickup.
4. **Pickup & Delivery:** Fleet drivers pick up the container from the terminal and deliver it safely to the destination.
5. **Empty Return:** After unloading, empty containers are returned to the designated yard/terminal prior to detention deadlines.

---

## 🛠️ The 4-Step Work Cycle

HutchFlow breaks down dispatch training into four core operational phases:

### **Phase 01: Orders**
- **Description:** Receive and enter client orders into the central database.
- **Focus:** Ensure accuracy in shipment details, customer information, and order parameters.

### **Phase 02: Appointments**
- **Description:** Coordinate and confirm delivery windows with consignees.
- **Focus:** Lock in precise delivery timing and review special site instructions before dispatching.

### **Phase 03: Dispatch**
- **Description:** Assign and dispatch loads to available drivers.
- **Focus:** Verify equipment/RV times, container numbers, and driver availability.

### **Phase 04: Accounts**
- **Description:** Manage billing, invoicing, and payment processing.
- **Focus:** Ensure all charges, fees, and payment references are accurately recorded and up to date.
